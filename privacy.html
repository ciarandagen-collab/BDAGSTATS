Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Coach Stats is operated by Ciaran Dagen, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

Data controller: Ciaran Dagen trading as Coach Stats

Contact: hello@coachstats.app

This app is used primarily in the United Kingdom and Ireland. UK GDPR applies. Where users are based in Ireland, EU GDPR also applies and the same protections are provided.

2. What Data We Collect

Account data: Your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase — passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text).

Match data: Match records you create including team names, scores, events, player names, notes and statistics. This data belongs to you.

Squad data: Player names, positions, attendance records, training notes, skill ratings, injury logs and availability you enter.

Wellness data: Player self-reported wellness scores submitted via the shared wellness link feature.

Payment data: Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details — Stripe handles all payment data under their own privacy policy.

Usage data: We do not use analytics tools or track your behaviour within the app beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Coach Stats service
  • To store and sync your match, squad and training data across devices
  • To process your subscription via Stripe
  • To respond to support requests sent to hello@coachstats.app
  • To send service-related emails (account creation, subscription receipts)

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests: Keeping the app secure and operational
  • Consent: Wellness data submitted by players via the shared link

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS). Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 certified. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following third parties, strictly to provide the service:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management
  • Netlify — app hosting and serverless functions
  • Cloudflare — DNS and email routing

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

7. Player Data & Wellness

If you use the wellness self-report feature, you share a link with your players. Players voluntarily submit their name and wellness scores. This data is stored in your account and visible only to you as the coach. Players should be informed before using this feature that their wellness data is being collected and stored.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Restriction — request we limit how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@coachstats.app. We will respond within 30 days.

You can also delete your account directly within the app at any time — this permanently removes all your data.

9. Cookies

Coach Stats is a Progressive Web App. We use browser localStorage to store your match data and preferences on your device. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party cookies.

10. Children

Coach Stats is intended for coaches and managers aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Player names entered by coaches may include underage players — this data is stored under the coach's account and is the coach's responsibility to manage appropriately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you by email if we make significant changes. The latest version is always available at coachstats.app/privacy.

12. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your data, contact us at hello@coachstats.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.
