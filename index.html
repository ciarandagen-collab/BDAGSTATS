Everything you need

Built for the sideline,
designed for analysis

Every feature a GAA coach needs — from the first whistle to the final review.

✍️
Live Match Recording
Three taps and it's logged: tap the event, the outcome, then the player. One button per team on every event, with a running tally on each. Nothing saves until you pick the player, so a mis-tap costs nothing.
📝
Written Match Analysis
Every report opens with plain findings, not just tables. "Losing more than a third of your own restarts hands them possession in good areas." Sentences you can take into training on Tuesday.
📡
Live Share
Hand the recording to someone else and follow the stats on your own phone. Share a six-character code and anyone can watch the score, possession and shooting update as it happens.
🦶
Kickout Analysis
Won clean, break won, short — and where it landed. Retention split by short, middle and long, and by left, centre and right channel, so you know exactly what to change at half time.
📐
Shot Quality
Weighs every shot by how often that kind of chance is converted, so you can tell bad shooting from bad shot selection. It learns your own conversion rates as the season goes on.
💸
Cost of Turnovers
Links a lost possession to the score that followed it. "Conceded 1-04 directly from turnovers" makes a careless hand pass feel like what it is.
🟨
Discipline Warnings
A player already on a yellow is ringed in amber on the pitch, for both teams, and warns you before you record whatever he has just done.
🎯
Shot Map
Tap the pitch to mark exactly where each shot came from, on a diagram drawn to proper GAA markings — 13m, 20m, 45m and the two-point arc. Colour coded by outcome and included in every PDF report.
👥
Squad Management
A 34-player panel with attendance, injuries, availability and performance scores. Season figures build automatically from saved matches, and each player is flagged reliable, variable or streaky.
🏃
Training & Wellness
Log every training session. Share a wellness link with your squad — players rate sleep, energy, soreness, stress and mood before training.
🧪
Player Trials
Run structured multi-week trials with 1–10 grading. Share a grading link with co-coaches. Rankings, final decisions and PDF report.
🎬
Video Analysis
Paste a YouTube link or upload a match file straight from your phone. Tag moments as you watch, build highlight clips by category and share the clip sheet.
📊
Season Summary
W/D/L record, KPI tiles, top performers and full results table. Generate a printable PDF management report for any season.
🏟
Opposition Database
Builds itself from your match history. Every opponent profiled with record, scout reports and notes — plus a picture of their players as you tag them, and where they rank among the sides you play.
📅
Fixture List
Schedule upcoming matches with date, time, opponent and venue. Links automatically to match records when played.
Simple to use

Up and running in minutes

No training needed. Open the app, set up your teams and start recording.

1
Install the App
Open coachstats.app in Safari on your iPhone or iPad. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Works like a native app, even offline.
2
Set Up Your Match
Tap the team names to search 1,200+ GAA clubs. Select your competition. Tap Start Game — the clock starts automatically.
3
Record & Analyse
Tap event buttons as they happen. After the game, view your shot map, share the report and build your season picture.
